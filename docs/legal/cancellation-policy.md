# Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 7 July 2026
**Last updated:** 7 July 2026

This Cancellation Policy explains how you can cancel your subscription to the ROCloud platform at
**https://rocloud.in** ("**Service**"), operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("**ROCloud**",
"**we**", "**us**", "**our**"). It should be read together with our
**[Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.md)** and **[Refund Policy](refund-policy.md)**.

---

## 1. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time by either:

- **In the app** — open **Settings → Subscription** in the owner portal and choose to cancel /
  not renew; or
- **By email** — write to **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** from your registered email address, requesting
  cancellation and including your account/business name.

We will confirm your cancellation by email.

## 2. What happens when you cancel

2.1 **Access continues until the end of your paid period.** Cancelling stops your subscription from
continuing into the next period. You keep access to your paid plan until the end of the billing
period you have already paid for.

2.2 **No automatic in-app renewal.** ROCloud does not auto-renew subscriptions within the app, so
cancelling primarily means you will not be prompted or charged to continue. After your paid period
ends, your account moves to an unpaid state and paid features become unavailable.

2.3 **No pro-rata refund.** Cancellation does not, by itself, entitle you to a refund for the unused
portion of the current period. Refunds are governed by the **[Refund Policy](refund-policy.md)**.

## 3. Downgrading instead of cancelling

If you only need fewer features, you can **downgrade** to a lower plan rather than cancel. A
downgrade takes effect from your next billing period; the price difference for the current period is
not refunded.

## 4. Your data after cancellation

4.1 Before your access ends, please **export any data you wish to keep** (for example, customer,
invoice, and payment records) using the export tools available in the Service.

4.2 After cancellation, we retain your Subscriber Data for a reasonable period so you can reactivate
without loss, after which it may be deleted in line with our
**[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)** and our data-retention practices. Some records may be
retained longer where required by law (for example, tax and accounting records).

4.3 If you want your data deleted sooner, contact us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and we will act on your
request subject to legal-retention requirements.

## 5. Reactivating

You can reactivate a cancelled or lapsed account at any time (subject to data still being available)
by signing in and renewing a plan in **Settings → Subscription**, or by contacting us.

## 6. Cancellation or suspension by us

We may suspend or cancel your account in accordance with the
**[Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.md)** — for example, for non-payment or breach. Where
practicable, we will give you notice and an opportunity to resolve the issue (such as by paying
outstanding fees).
